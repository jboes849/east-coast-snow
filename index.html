How this forecast works

Most snow apps show one model's number. This site pulls five (GFS/HRRR, ECMWF, Canadian, German ICON, and the National Weather Service blend) and shows where they agree and where they don't.

Snow totals use a temperature-based snow-to-liquid ratio at each resort's summit elevation, so cold storms come out fluffier and warm ones heavier, instead of the fixed ratio most models use. The powder score weights snow that falls overnight before first chair, cold temperatures, no rain, and model agreement.
